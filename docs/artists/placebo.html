Placebo — Running Up That Hill

Played: 2026-07-30 (Moment in History)

Placebo released their cover of Kate Bush's "Running Up That Hill (A Deal with God)" in 2003. Brian Molko has said the band chose to cover it because "we've always been massive Kate Bush fans," and he connected deeply with the song's theme of wanting to swap places with a loved one to truly understand them. Placebo's version originally appeared as a B-side and on their B-sides/rarities collection Covers. Two decades later, when Kate Bush's original surged to #1 worldwide in 2022 after featuring in Stranger Things Season 4, Placebo's cover also experienced renewed interest. The band released a live performance video of their cover in June 2022 to meet the demand.

  • Moment in History: Kate Bush, whose song this is a cover of, was born on 30 July 1958 — the same calendar date as this show. She had been out of the public eye for over a decade since her 2011 album 50 Words for Snow until 2022, when Stranger Things Season 4's use of "Running Up That Hill" sent the original to #1 on the Billboard Global 200 — the same resurgence referenced in this track's own research.
