12. The Weeknd — Blinding Lights

Release date: 20 March 2020 Track: 9 of 14 Key / BPM: Eb / 86

Blinding Lights became one of the most streamed songs in history, spending 57 weeks in the US Billboard Hot 100 top ten — a record at the time.

Built on an eighties synth-pop aesthetic. Influences cited include Michael Jackson, Bruce Springsteen, and Phil Collins.

Sits at the centre of the synth bloc alongside Witch Post, Sjowgren, and James Blake — four tracks sharing a nocturnal electronic mood.
