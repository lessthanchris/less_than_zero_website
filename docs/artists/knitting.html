knitting — I Wasn't Fully Cooked

Played: 2026-06-04 (Segment 1 — Atmospheric open)

knitting are a Montreal-based alt-rock band on Mint Records. "I Wasn't Fully Cooked" is the third single from their forthcoming debut album Souvenir, due June 26, 2026. The track is heavy and raw: fuzzy guitars, pounding drums, a cathartic intensity. Mischa Dempsey wrote it from touring fears in early 2020 — a fear of dying before fully becoming themselves, describing it as "a way for me to think about what death interrupts and what gets remembered." A music video directed by Sasha Khalimonova was released alongside the single. Closes the opening segment, bringing the dreamy atmosphere back to earth before the show shifts into the singer-songwriter run.
