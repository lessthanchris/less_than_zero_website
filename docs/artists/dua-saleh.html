08. Dua Saleh — Cállate

Dua Saleh is a Sudanese-American non-binary artist from Minneapolis. Their early EPs Rosebud (2019) and ROSETTA (2020) established them in a space between R&B, indie, and experimental pop. "Cállate" (released May 15, 2026) is the lead single from the forthcoming album Of Earth & Wires.
