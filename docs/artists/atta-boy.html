Atta Boy — Full Cloud

Played: 2026-06-04 (Segment 5 — Punk run) (new release)

Atta Boy are a Los Angeles band: Eden Brolin, Lewis Pullman, Freddy Reish, and Dashel Thompson, friends since middle school, working across time zones for years before reuniting at Reish's Pink Feather studio in LA to make their album Silt. "Full Cloud" is the lead single. The band describe it as being about "the uncertainty of trying to communicate, the comfort of history, and the feeling of returning to something familiar after time apart" — describing the band's own process as much as anything else. Closes segment five on a more personal note; a codependency theme ("suffocated by your own codependence") adds an edge to what sounds like jangly, energetic indie rock.
