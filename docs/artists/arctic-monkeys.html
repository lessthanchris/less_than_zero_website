Arctic Monkeys — Opening Night

Played: 2026-01-29 (new release)

Arctic Monkeys have returned with a new single released in collaboration with War Child. Written by Alex Turner and produced by James Ford, 'Opening Night' was recorded at Abbey Road and serves as the lead single from the charity's upcoming compilation 'Help(2)' - a modern nod to 1995's 'The Help' album. Released via Domino, the song marks a rare and meaningful release from the band in support of War Child's work.
