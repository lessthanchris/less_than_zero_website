Alvvays — Archie, Marry Me

Played: 2026-09-03

The jangly, C86-indebted love song released as the advance single from Alvvays' 2014 self-titled debut, instantly the band's calling card and a touchstone of modern indie pop. Penned by Molly Rankin with guitarist Alec O'Hanley, it's a marriage proposal addressed to a fictional "Archie," delivered over fuzz-laden guitars and a sunny chorus. Certified Gold a full decade after release, in December 2024 — a fittingly slow-burn run for a song about chilly commitment — and it regularly lands on Rolling Stone and other all-time indie-song lists.

Alvvays

  • Origin: Charlottetown, Prince Edward Island, Canada (based Toronto, Ontario) · formed 2011 · active
  • Members: Molly Rankin (vocals, guitar), Alec O'Hanley (guitar), Kerri MacLellan (keyboards), plus rotating bass/drums (Brian Murphy and Phil MacIsaac on early records)
  • Genre: Indie pop, jangle pop, dream pop
  • Discography:
  • Alvvays (2014) — Polyvinyl / Royal Mountain — self-titled debut, topped US college charts, spawned "Archie, Marry Me"
  • Antisocialites (2017) — Polyvinyl / Royal Mountain — won Juno Award for Alternative Album of the Year
  • Blue Rev (2022) — Polyvinyl / Royal Mountain — won Juno Award for Alternative Album of the Year
  • Facts:
  • Molly Rankin is the daughter of John Morris Rankin of the celebrated Canadian folk group the Rankin Family.
  • Rankin and O'Hanley started writing together after meeting at a teenage gig, later self-releasing a solo EP before forming the full band.
