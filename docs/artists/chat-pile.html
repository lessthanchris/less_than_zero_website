Chat Pile — Creature

Played: 2026-09-10

Chat Pile are a noise-rock/sludge band from Oklahoma City: Raygun Busch (vocals), Luther (guitar), Stin (bass), Cap'n Ron (drums). "Creature" (4:23) is the album-opening track 1 on Who Loves the Sun, released 4 September 2026 on The Flenser — arriving six days before this show. It opened the album cycle and was previewed ahead of release. The band broke through with the dual 2019 EPs This Dungeon Earth and Remove Your Skin Please, then released God's Country (2022) and Cool World (2024). Bassist Stin said the new record leans toward classic alt/indie rock: "Luther's guitar work is the most XTC-sounding it's ever been, and Raygun is definitely approaching this album with more hooks in mind." The album's lyrical targets include climate change, modern work culture and the over-optimisation of data. A second vinyl pressing on black, gold and baby-blue merge vinyl (1,000 copies) was scheduled to ship around 25 September 2026.
