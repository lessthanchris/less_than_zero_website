Bedhead Loved Macha

  • Origin: Wichita Falls, Texas (Bedhead) / Athens, Georgia (Macha) · one-off collaboration, released 2001
  • Members: A joint project of Bedhead's Matt and Bubba Kadane and Macha's Josh and Mischo McKay
  • Genre: Slowcore, gamelan-influenced experimental pop
  • Discography:
  • Macha Loved Bedhead / Bedhead Loved Macha (2001, reissued 2026) — Numero Group — five-song, 34-minute long-distance collaboration EP, includes the cover "Believe"
  • Facts:
  • Bedhead had broken up in 1998; the Kadane brothers reunited specifically for this one-off project with Macha a couple of years later.
  • Their cover of Cher's "Believe" strips the original's bombastic Auto-Tune hook down to a hushed, touch-tone-phone rendition — described as turning the song's "sadness and desperation up to 11."
