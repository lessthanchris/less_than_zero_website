Alabama Shakes

  • Origin: Athens, Alabama, USA · formed 2009 · active (reunited December 2024 after a 2018–2024 hiatus)
  • Members: Brittany Howard — lead vocals, guitar; Heath Fogg — guitar, backing vocals; Zac Cockrell — bass. (Original drummer Steve Johnson did not rejoin after the reunion, following legal troubles during the hiatus.)
  • Genre: Blues rock, Southern rock, soul, roots rock, garage rock

  • I Must Be Dreaming (2026) — Island Records — first album in over a decade
  • Facts:
  • Brittany Howard and Zac Cockrell met in an advanced psychology class at East Limestone High School in Athens, Alabama, and began writing songs together after school.

  • During the hiatus, Howard released two acclaimed solo albums, Jaime (2019) and What Now (2024).
