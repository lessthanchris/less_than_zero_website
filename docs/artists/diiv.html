DIIV — The Fountain

Played: 2026-08-13 (Daily Music Report)

DIIV are a US band (New York) formed 2011 by Zachary Cole Smith (formerly of Beach Fossils). "The Fountain" (3:25) is the lead single and track 1 of 11 on ZIRP!, DIIV's fifth studio album (following Frog in Boiling Water, 2024), due 30 October 2026 on Fantasy Recordings, produced by A.G. Cook. Cook told The Line of Best Fit: "I became a bit obsessed with their live sound, and insisted on recording live drums before most of the songs were even finished." The album was first teased the prior week via a hotline card mailed to fans. Full tracklist: "The Fountain," "Kid," "When The Glow Came For Us," "Dare Me," "The Crash," "Trillions," "Us Against You," "Evangelizer," "Underground," "My Star," "Idiot In The Sky." Daily Music Report note: the track blends DIIV's signature shoegaze with a new electronic edge — whispery vocals and ominous guitars over oscillating synths and woozy lead guitar — opening the album as the band pushes into more experimental territory.

DIIV

  • Origin: Brooklyn, New York, US · formed 2011 · active
  • Members: Zachary Cole Smith (vocals, guitar), Andrew Bailey (guitar), Colin Caulfield (bass, keyboards, guitar, vocals), Ben Newman (drums)
  • Genre: Shoegaze, dream pop, neo-psychedelia, post-punk
  • Discography:
  • Oshin (2012) — Captured Tracks — debut, blended krautrock, post-punk, and shoegaze
  • Is the Is Are (2016) — Captured Tracks
  • Deceiver (2019) — Captured Tracks
  • Frog in Boiling Water (2024) — Fantasy Recordings
  • ZIRP! (2026) — Fantasy Recordings — produced by A.G. Cook, due 30 October 2026
  • Facts:
  • The project was originally named "Dive" after the Nirvana song, later respelled DIIV to avoid confusion with an unrelated Belgian band called Dive.
  • Frontman Zachary Cole Smith previously played guitar in Beach Fossils before founding DIIV.
