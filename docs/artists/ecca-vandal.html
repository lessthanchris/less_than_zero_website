05. Ecca Vandal — SORRY! CRASH!

Ecca Vandal is South African-born, Melbourne-based — working across punk, hip-hop, and jazz. SORRY! CRASH! is the lead single from her album Looking for People to Unfollow (Loma Vista, May 22, 2026 — out tomorrow). It's her first album since 2017. Recorded and produced with Richie Buxton in his childhood bedroom in bayside Melbourne. The album is 17 tracks; its central theme is subtraction — letting go of what drains your energy, and independence from social media and trends. SORRY! CRASH! captures the strange clarity of finally admitting you're not okay. Racing percussion, throbbing bass, face-melting guitar.
