05. The Smashing Pumpkins — 1979

Release date: October 1995 Track: 5 of disc 2 Key / BPM: Eb / 127

1979 appears on the Twilight to Starlight disc of the Smashing Pumpkins' double album. It stands apart — lighter, hazy, propelled by a drum machine loop rather than Jimmy Chamberlin's usual thunderous playing.

Billy Corgan has said the song is about the nostalgia and aimlessness of teenage years. 1979 is the year Corgan was born.

The production strips back the band's usual wall of sound into something intimate and wistful. Nearly thirty years later, still a fan favourite.

The Smashing Pumpkins

  • Origin: Chicago, Illinois, USA · formed 1988 · active (broke up 2000, reunited 2006)
  • Members: Billy Corgan (vocals, guitar, keyboards, bass — sole constant member), James Iha (guitar), Jimmy Chamberlin (drums); original bassist D'arcy Wretzky is not part of the current lineup
