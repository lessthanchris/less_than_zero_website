Chinese American Bear — Dance Dance Dance (跳舞跳舞跳舞)

Played: 2026-09-03 (Daily Music Report) (TikTok)

Daily Music Report pick from Michael Lutjen: a groovy, riff-heavy disco track with psychedelic touches, built around a bass-and-drum groove that practically demands movement; blending English and Mandarin in its playful chorus, it embraces the simple joy of letting loose and following the beat. A single from the Seattle-based Mando-pop duo of Anne Tong and Bryce Barsten, who wear their "experimental indie c-pop" tag proudly. Released late August 2026 alongside a video and ahead of their EU tour, it's actually an outtake rescued from the sessions for their most recent album rather than a fresh recording. Populist, call-and-response and bilingual (hence three stacked "dance" characters in the Chinese title), it's being championed as one of the best songs of its week. Fun fact: this track is what led Michael Lutjen to discover The Daily Music Report.

Chinese American Bear

  • Origin: Seattle, Washington, USA · formed 2020 · active
  • Members: Lingbo Anne Tong (vocals, keyboards, gong), Bryce Barsten (vocals, keyboards, guitar) — married couple
  • Genre: C-pop / neo-psychedelic pop
  • Discography:
  • Chinese American Bear (2022) — Modern Sky
  • Wah!!! (2024) — Moshi Moshi Records — named among The Seattle Times' best Washington albums of the year
  • Dim Sum & Then Some (2026)
  • Facts:
  • Tong and Barsten met in high school jazz band in Spokane; Tong studied economics at Princeton and holds an MBA from the University of Chicago, while Barsten was raised on a llama farm.
  • They sing in a mix of Mandarin and English, and their raucous live shows feature audience sing-alongs, a costumed dancer, and plushie giveaways.
