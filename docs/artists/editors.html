21. Editors — Smokers Outside the Hospital Doors

Editors are a post-punk and indie rock band from Birmingham, formed in 2002. Frontman Tom Smith leads a sound that draws heavily on late-70s and 80s post-punk — Joy Division, Wire, Echo & the Bunnymen. Their debut The Back Room (2005) contained "Munich" and "Blood." An End Has a Start (2007) was their second album, and "Smokers Outside the Hospital Doors" opens it. The song takes a specific, mundane image — patients in hospital gowns gathered at the entrance to smoke — and finds something larger inside it. It charted in the UK on its single release.

Placement: TikTok opener for Segment 7, the close. Editors pull the energy back down from Segment 6's sprint. The track's familiarity in indie rock circles gives the close a sense of arrival — something known and reliable after a show full of new music.
