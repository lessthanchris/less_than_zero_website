16. A Certain Ratio — Naked and White (FM)

A Certain Ratio are a post-punk and funk band from Manchester, formed in 1977 on Factory Records. Contemporaries of Joy Division and New Order, central to the Manchester scene but without the same commercial reach. Their sound mixes post-punk angularity with Afrobeat percussion and bass-driven funk — a combination that influenced a generation of British bands. "Naked and White (FM)" is a single released ahead of their album Force Majeure, due August 28, 2026. 3:59 at 128 BPM.
