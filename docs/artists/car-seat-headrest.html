09. Car Seat Headrest — Drunk Drivers/Killer Whales

Car Seat Headrest is Will Toledo, from Leesburg, Virginia. Teens of Denial (2016, Matador) was his commercial and critical breakthrough. Teen of Denial: Joe's Story was released May 20, 2026 — a 10th-anniversary re-imagining of the album reframed around a character called Joe. Toledo returned to the studio with original producer Steve Fisk to tweak lyrics, revise arrangements, and write new songs including "Ravenous House" and "Joe Drives Again." The re-recording was controversial: some critics argued the original needed no revision. "Drunk Drivers/Killer Whales" is one of the re-recorded tracks, extended to 6:10.

Car Seat Headrest — Sober to Death

Played: 2026-06-04 (Segment 6 — Late night) (TikTok)

Car Seat Headrest is the project of Will Toledo, a Virginia-born musician who recorded his early albums alone in his car and posted them to Bandcamp. Twin Fantasy was recorded when Toledo was 18-19 and released in November 2011 as a digital-only Bandcamp album — the sixth in a run of self-released records made before he was signed. "Sober to Death" is track four: begins as a finger-picked acoustic song before opening into a raw pop-rock anthem, built around the lyric "We were wrecks before we crashed into each other." Toledo later re-recorded the entire album as Twin Fantasy (Mirror to Mirror) in 2018 — full band, rewritten lyrics, remixed and expanded. This is the original: lo-fi, teenage, unfiltered. Opens segment six, its rawness pairing well with what follows — Laura Cahen's French chanson and Angèle's late-night electronic rework.

  • TikTok angle: Will Toledo recorded Twin Fantasy at 19, alone in his car — where the band name Car Seat Headrest came from — and posted it to Bandcamp in 2011.
