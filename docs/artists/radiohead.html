Radiohead — Killer Cars (Mogadon version)

Played: 2026-07-15 (Segment 3) (TikTok)

Radiohead (formed 1985, Abingdon, Oxfordshire) released "Killer Cars (Mogadon Version)" (3:50) as a B-side on the "Just" single (21 August 1995, XL Recordings/Parlophone) — "Just" reached No. 19 on the UK Singles Chart. A separate, non-Mogadon version of the song appears on the "High & Dry / Planet Telex" single. The drum loop from "Killer Cars" became the foundation for "Planet Telex" on The Bends, which was written and recorded in a single evening at RAK Studios.

  • TikTok angle: Deep-dive/trivia angle: the track is named after Mogadon, a brand name for the sedative nitrazepam, which fits its down-beat, meditative sound — pitched as a 1995 deep cut most listeners haven't heard.
