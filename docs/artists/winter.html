Winter — without you (mixtape version)

Played: 2026-08-13

Winter is the project of Samira Winter, a Brazilian-born, Los Angeles-based shoegaze singer-songwriter and guitarist. "Without You" (mixtape version, 3:23), featuring Soccer Mommy (Sophie Allison), appears on the Romantix Mixtape EP, scheduled for digital release via Winspear on 18 September 2026. Written by Samira Winter and Alex Craig. "Without You" originally appeared on Winter's 2025 LP Adult Romantix, announced May 2025 and described as a goodbye letter to her time in Los Angeles; the mixtape is a companion release to that album, per Winter's Bandcamp and Winspear label pages.
