Chalk — Get Fucked

Played: 2026-07-15 (Segment 6) (TikTok)

Chalk is a Belfast dance-punk/industrial duo (Ross Cullen — vocals, Benedict Goddard) who coined the term "Crystalpunk" for their sound. "Get Fucked" is a standalone single released 3 July 2026 on ALTER Music LLC, following their debut album Crystalpunk (13 March 2026), which received a five-star NME review and reached No. 1 on the UK Dance Album Chart. It was produced with Irish producer Kettama (Evan); the band told NME (2 July 2026): "It sat for a while until we worked on it with Evan, who gave it a new lease of life." Their music addresses the Protestant-Catholic, English-Irish heritage of post-Troubles Belfast (The Guardian, 18 March 2026).
