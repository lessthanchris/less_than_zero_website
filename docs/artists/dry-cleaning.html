London post-punk group. Florence Shaw's spoken-word delivery is one of the most distinctive voices in British indie — flat, precise, building meaning through accumulation rather than emphasis. Explores visibility and anonymity — the discomfort of being seen, the desire to disappear — with surreal imagery and quiet instrumentation. Opens the final double-header with Lambrini Girls.

Dry Cleaning

  • Origin: London, England · formed 2017 · active
  • Members: Florence Shaw (vocals/lyrics), Tom Dowse (guitar), Lewis Maynard (bass), Nick Buxton (drums)
  • Genre: Post-punk, spoken word
  • Discography:
  • Sweet Princess (EP, 2019) — It's OK — self-released 2018, reissued

  • Facts:
  • Florence Shaw was recruited as vocalist in 2018 after the other members recorded her speaking over their instrumentals during a rehearsal — she had no prior singing/performing background, working as a visual artist and lecturer at the time.
  • Shaw's lyrics are built from overheard speech, found text, and stream-of-consciousness notes rather than conventional songwriting.
