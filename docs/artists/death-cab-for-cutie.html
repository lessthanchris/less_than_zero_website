Death Cab For Cutie — Riptides

Played: 2026-03-19

Track 9 and lead single from Death Cab For Cutie's 11th studio album I Built You a Tower, released 16 March 2026 on ANTI-; album due 5 June 2026 — their first release on an independent label after more than 20 years on Atlantic. Produced by John Congleton and recorded in three weeks at Animal Rites in Los Angeles as well as the band members' homes in Seattle, Portland, Bellingham, and LA. The album grew from the band's 20th anniversary tours for Transatlanticism and Plans (2023-2025), during which frontman Ben Gibbard was privately dealing with the collapse of his marriage. Gibbard: 'Riptides is about the challenge of dealing with personal struggles as the world around us experiences tragedy and loss on an unfathomable scale.' The band also announced a UK/European tour including Manchester, Edinburgh, and Bristol.

Death Cab For Cutie — Transatlanticism (demo)

Played: 2026-07-15 (Segment 10 — Close) (Closer)

Death Cab for Cutie (formed 1997, Bellingham, WA; 2003 lineup: Ben Gibbard, Chris Walla, Nick Harmer, Jason McGerr, who joined for this album) closed their fourth album Transatlanticism (7 October 2003, Barsuk Records) with a demo version of the title track (6:13). The album was produced by Chris Walla at Hall of Justice Studio in Seattle, reached No. 97 on the Billboard 200, and was certified Gold by the RIAA.
