19. Butthole Surfers — Jet Fighter

Release date: Single March 2026, album 26 June 2026 Track: 3 of 12 Key / BPM: F / 100

After the Astronaut was recorded around 1997 as the follow-up to Electriclarryland. Capitol Records declined to release it — not commercial enough.

Capitol dropped the band. Hollywood Records (a Disney subsidiary) bought the tapes and released a reworked version as Weird Revolution in 2001.

The original sat unreleased for nearly thirty years. Out 26 June 2026 on Sunset Blvd. Records, exactly as intended.

Jet Fighter is the lead single. Paul Leary: it came together when he bought a 12-string electric guitar and wanted to play it. Anti-war thread, character named Mikey.

Surf-punk, lo-fi, dadaist psych rock. Thirty years late and not remotely sorry.
