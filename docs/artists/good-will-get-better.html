good will get better — Bones (are their money)

good will, get better are a self-described "West coast emo/pop punk" project from San Bernardino, California. "Bones (are their money)" is a standalone single (3:00), self-released on Bandcamp 9 August 2024, and does not appear on the 2025 album It's All Going According to Plan. The band's debut album Getting There followed on streaming in 2024; It's All Going According to Plan was released July 2025. The band has roughly 13,000 monthly listeners on Spotify as of September 2026. The frontman has said on social media he spent eight months on one song (the album's "WDWGILY"), per a May 2024 Instagram reel.
