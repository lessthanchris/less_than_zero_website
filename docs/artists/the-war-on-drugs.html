The War On Drugs — Red Eyes

Played: 2026-09-03 (TikTok)

Lead single from the 2014 breakthrough Lost in the Dream, released December 2013 and widely credited with pushing the band from cult favourite to festival headliner. A four-minute rush of billowing synths, chugging guitars and Adam Granduciel's husky, Dylan-adjacent vocal, fusing Springsteen-style heartland rock with the motorik pulse of Krautrock. The album was recorded during a rough patch for Granduciel, who has spoken publicly about battling anxiety and insomnia, and the song's forward-surging energy works as a defiant counterweight to that restlessness.
