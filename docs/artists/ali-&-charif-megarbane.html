07. Ali & Charif Megarbane — Mosaics

Release date: 20 March 2026 Track: 3 of 13 Key / BPM: Eb / 129

Tirakat brings together Lebanese composer Charif Megarbane and Ali, a Jakarta-based trio. Recorded in just three days in Berlin, driven by intuition, repetition, and trust.

Rooted in the long-standing cultural exchange between Indonesia and the Arab world. Both artists approached it as music that already felt interconnected rather than a fusion project.

The title comes from the Javanese practice of discipline and devotion, derived from the Arabic tariqa meaning "path." Released on Habibi Funk, the Berlin label dedicated to sounds from the Arab world.

Only 300 copies pressed on coke green transparent vinyl. Sold out immediately.
