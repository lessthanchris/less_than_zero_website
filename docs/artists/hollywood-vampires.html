Hollywood Vampires are a US rock supergroup of Alice Cooper, Johnny Depp, Joe Perry and Tommy Henriksen, named after the celebrity drinking club Cooper formed in the 1970s, with two studio albums: Hollywood Vampires (2015) and Rise (2019)

Hollywood Vampires

  • Origin: Los Angeles, US · formed 2012 · active
  • Members: Alice Cooper (vocals), Johnny Depp (guitar), Joe Perry (guitar, Aerosmith), Tommy Henriksen (guitar/bass) — plus a revolving cast of guest musicians
  • Genre: Hard rock / classic rock covers & originals
  • Discography:
  • Hollywood Vampires (2015) — Republic Records — mostly covers, guests include Dave Grohl, Slash, Brian Johnson, Joe Walsh
  • Rise (2019) — earMUSIC — mostly original material
  • Live in Rio (2022) — first live album
  • Facts:
  • The band's name and concept revive "The Hollywood Vampires," a real 1970s celebrity drinking club Alice Cooper formed at LA's Rainbow Bar and Grill with John Lennon, Harry Nilsson, Keith Moon and Ringo Starr.
  • The current lineup's original albums are dedicated to musicians who died from drug or alcohol abuse, including several former members of the original 1970s Vampires club.

"My Dead Drunk Friends" (Live at Montreux Jazz Festival) (4:38) is track 5 on At Montreux Jazz Festival (Live), released 14 August 2026 on earMUSIC — recorded at the Montreux Jazz Festival in 2018, announced 1 July 2026. The song originally appeared on the band's 2015 self-titled debut album. The band take their name from the Hollywood Vampires drinking club formed by Alice Cooper in the 1970s; hazing to get in was to outdrink all the members. The 19-track live set also includes covers — "I Got a Line on You", Love's "7 And 7 Is", and Doors medleys ("Five To One/Break On Through").
