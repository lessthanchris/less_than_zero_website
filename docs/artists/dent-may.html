The video single (video out 25 June 2026) comes from Dent May's brand-new album The Big One, released 14 August 2026 on Carpark Records (CAK188), the week it hits. The title references 'the long-anticipated natural disaster,' and May has made the mortality angle explicit: 'The biggest of the big ones is, of course, death... in the face of knowing that everything will come crashing down sooner or later, are you going to be okay?'
