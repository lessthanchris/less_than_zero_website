17. Nine Inch Nails — Heresy (Boys Noize Remix)

Originally from The Downward Spiral (1994), Nine Inch Nails' second studio album. The Downward Spiral is one of the defining industrial rock records — a concept album about self-destruction, recorded in the house where the Tate murders took place. Trent Reznor has said the location was chosen deliberately, and that he became unsettled by it during the sessions.

This version is a collaboration between Trent Reznor, Atticus Ross, and Boys Noize — reimagined as a grittier, slowed-down take that strips the track into something heavier, more abrasive, and more atmospheric. The original's industrial fury reshaped into a darker form.

Sits here as the intensity peak of the punk run before the closing segment.
