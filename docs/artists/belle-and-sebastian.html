Belle and Sebastian — It Only Takes One Lion

Played: 2026-06-04 (Segment 3 — Indie push)

Belle and Sebastian are a Glasgow indie pop band formed in 1994, led by Stuart Murdoch, twelve studio albums, one of Scotland's most enduring exports. "It Only Takes One Lion" was released June 2, 2026 via Matador Records — a Scotland World Cup anthem written following Scotland's 4-2 qualifying win against Denmark; Murdoch wrote it the day after the match. Premiered at London's Royal Albert Hall as part of the band's world tour celebrating the anniversary of Tigermilk and If You're Feeling Sinister. Moves from hopeful hymn to uptempo, string-laden disco pop, co-written and produced with Pete Ferguson. Murdoch: "The song tries to encompass the experience of the whole country following Scotland." Middle of segment three — the Glasgow connection is a nice handshake, the city that made them now rallying behind the national team.
