18. The Mountain Goats — Charlie Sheen Reaches Out to the Feds

The Mountain Goats is primarily the project of John Darnielle, based in Durham, North Carolina. Active since the early 1990s, one of the most extensive and consistently literary catalogues in indie music — All Hail West Texas (2002), Tallahassee (2002), The Sunset Tree (2005) are touchstones. "Charlie Sheen Reaches Out to the Feds" is the lead single from their 24th album Days, due August 7, 2026. The backstory: in 1991 Charlie Sheen watched the Japanese horror film Guinea Pig 2: Flower of Flesh and Blood and rang the FBI convinced it was a real snuff film. Produced by John Congleton at Sear Sound in Manhattan, with Rob Jost (bass and French horn), Janis Siegal of Manhattan Transfer on backing vocals, and harpist Mikaela Davis. Slightly funky, oddly anthemic. 3:12 at 150 BPM.

The Mountain Goats

  • Origin: Claremont, California, USA · formed 1991 · active
  • Members: John Darnielle (vocals, guitar, keyboard — founding/core member), Jon Wurster (drums), Matt Douglas (saxophone, flute, clarinet, guitar, bass, keyboard, backing vocals)
  • Genre: Indie folk, indie rock, folk rock, lo-fi
  • Discography (selected):
  • All Hail West Texas (2002) — self-released — peak of the boombox lo-fi era
  • Tallahassee (2002) — 4AD — first full-studio-band album, centers the "Alpha Couple" song cycle
  • The Sunset Tree (2005) — 4AD
  • Beat the Champ (2015) — Merge Records
  • Jenny from Thebes (2023) — Merge Records
  • Days (2026) — Cadmean Dawn — first release on the band's own label
  • Facts:
  • For most of the 1990s "The Mountain Goats" meant John Darnielle alone, recording direct to a cassette boombox despite the plural band name.
  • After fifteen years on Merge Records, the band now self-releases through their own label, Cadmean Dawn.
