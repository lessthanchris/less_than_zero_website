Måneskin — Beggin'

Played: 2026-06-25 (Segment 5 — Moment in History) (TikTok) (moment in history)

The Rome band's cover (originally The Four Seasons, 1967), from Chosen (2017), which became a vast TikTok hit. Måneskin actually recorded their version in 2017, but it didn't take off until they won Eurovision in 2021. Connection not announced on air.

  • Moment in History: On 25 June 2021, Måneskin became the first Italian act ever to hold two simultaneous UK top-10 singles — "I Wanna Be Your Slave" (#6) and "Beggin'" (#10) — following their 2021 Eurovision win.

  • TikTok angle: "Beggin'" is a cover of The Four Seasons' 1967 original, recorded by Måneskin in 2017 but only exploding after their 2021 Eurovision win. On this exact date in 2021, they became the first Italian band in history to have two songs in the UK top ten simultaneously — this was one of them.

Måneskin

  • Origin: Rome, Italy · formed 2016 · on hiatus since 2024 (reunion performance planned for 2027)
  • Members: Damiano David (lead vocals, occasional guitar), Victoria De Angelis (bass, backing vocals), Thomas Raggi (guitars), Ethan Torchio (drums, percussion, guitars)
  • Genre: Pop rock, alternative rock, glam rock, hard rock, funk rock
  • Discography:
  • Il ballo della vita (2018) — Sony Music
  • Teatro d'ira: Vol. I (2021) — RCA/Sony
  • Rush! (2023) — Epic/Sony
  • Facts:
  • Won Eurovision 2021 with "Zitti e buoni" — the first rock song to win the contest since Lordi in 2006.
  • Became the first Italian rock band with three simultaneous UK top-10 entries in 2021, a wave that carried their 2017 cover of "Beggin'" to global hit status.
