Interpol — Wake Up

Played: 2026-09-03 (Daily Music Report) (TikTok)

Daily Music Report pick from Michael Lutjen: pairs Interpol's brooding post-punk foundation with sputtering drum loops and an orchestral deconstruction that gradually pulls the song apart, one of the more atmospheric moments on This Mirror Weighs a Ton, hinting at the experimentation running beneath the band's familiar sound. Closes out the first half of Interpol's eighth studio album This Mirror Weighs a Ton (released 28 August 2026 on Partisan Records), their first release since 2022's The Other Side of Make-Believe. Produced by Andrew Wyatt (of Miike Snow) and mixed by Dave Fridmann, recorded at Wyatt's New York City studio — the first time in a decade the band recorded in their home state, and their debut on a new label following a long stint on Matador. Longtime touring bassist Brad Truax was bumped up to full member for the record, while Wyatt played all the keyboards and synths; the album cover is Addie Wagenknecht's work from the Whitney Museum's permanent collection. Michael Lutjen called the album "impeccable."
