2026-07-30

"Roses" is the opening track on Sam Beam's 2026 album Hen's Teeth, released 28 February 2026 via Sub Pop. Beam described it as "a nice opening handshake that tells a weird story" — the album as a whole is "earthier, darker, more robust and more tactile" than its predecessor Light Verse (2024). Beam recorded Hen's Teeth by bringing skeletal song ideas to intuitive musicians and delighting in what emerged, a process driven by the musical liberation he found where folk and jazz meet. The album was noted by Uncut for its Southern melancholy and by critics for its Stephen Stills-like qualities. The title "Hen's Teeth" refers to something exceedingly rare.
