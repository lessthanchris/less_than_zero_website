Out 14 August 2026 — six days before this show — it's Bridgers' first solo studio album in six years, since 2020's Punisher, via Dead Oceans [unverified — Wikipedia only]. The album is themed around grief and the death of her father in 2023, co-produced by Tony Berg and Ethan Gruska with Jack Antonoff and Alex G, with guests including Bo Burnham, Cameron Winter, and Boygenius bandmates Lucy Dacus and Julien Baker [unverified — Wikipedia only].
