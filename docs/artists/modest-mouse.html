"Float On" is the huge crossover single from Good News for People Who Love Bad News (2004) — arguably Modest Mouse's most recognisable song. This Dan the Automator remix version appears on the album's 20th Anniversary Expanded Edition, giving a familiar song a different production texture for listeners who know the original well.
