03. Girl Scout — Simple Life

Release date: 20 March 2026 Track: 8 of 13 Key / BPM: G / 104

Stockholm trio who self-released their debut album on their own Human Garbage Records imprint. Mixed by Alex Farrar (Wednesday, Snail Mail).

The band call their sound "bubblegrunge" — guitar-driven indie rock with pop brightness and a grunge undercurrent. They toured with Alvvays before finishing the record.

Simple Life is vocalist Emma Jansson's personal favourite: "It was so easy to make. I wrote it, made a demo, didn't rehearse it, did it live in the studio and it was done." A song about the temptation to make yourself smaller.

Brink is a record about standing on the edge of adulthood, change, and decisions that now carry real weight.
