Bob Dylan — Maggie's Farm

Played: 2026-03-06

Album: Bringing It All Back Home | Released: 1965 | Label: Columbia Records. A protest song that marked Dylan's shift from acoustic folk to electric rock. He famously performed it electric at the 1965 Newport Folk Festival, an event that became one of the most controversial moments in music history when folk purists booed. The song is widely interpreted as a rejection of conformity and exploitation. It has been covered by many artists, including Rage Against the Machine. The title has been adopted as political shorthand in British politics.

Bob Dylan — Sara (Album Version)

Played: 2026-09-10 (TikTok)

"Sara" (5:31) closes Desire (track 10), co-written largely with Jacques Levy and produced by Don DeVito, released 5 January 1976 on Columbia Records. It was recorded on 31 July 1975, the final day of the Desire sessions. It is Dylan's direct address to his then-wife Sara Dylan, amid their divorce. Sara Dylan showed up at the studio and listened while Dylan recorded the song, per Jacques Levy's account in Howard Sounes's Dylan biography. Like "Visions of Johanna" a decade earlier, it is named for Sara — American Songwriter notes it would be the last song he would write for her in this direct register.

  • TikTok angle: Dylan almost never explains his songs, but Sara is the rare one he's admitted is about his then-wife — written as a plea during their divorce, and he recorded the vocal in one take while she watched. The most private song in the Dylan catalogue. Closer energy.
