Dinosaur Jr.

  • Origin: Amherst, Massachusetts, USA · formed 1984 · active (reunited 2005)
  • Members: J Mascis (guitar, vocals — sole continuous member), Lou Barlow (bass, vocals), Murph (Emmett Murphy, drums)
  • Genre: Alternative rock, noise rock, indie rock
  • Discography:
  • Dinosaur (1985) — Homestead Records — debut, released before the band added "Jr." to their name
  • You're Living All Over Me (1987) — SST Records — landmark alt-rock album
  • Bug (1988) — SST Records — last with original lineup before Barlow's 1989 departure
  • There Near (2026) — Jagjaguwar — 13th studio album
  • Facts:
  • Formed by J Mascis out of the ashes of his hardcore band Deep Wound; had to add "Jr." to their name in 1986 after being sued by an older band called Dinosaur (featuring ex-members of Jefferson Airplane).
  • Barlow was fired in 1989 (going on to form Sebadoh) and Murph left in 1991; Mascis retired the name in 1997 before the original trio reunited in 2005 and has recorded together since.
