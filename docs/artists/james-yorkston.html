James Yorkston

  • Origin: Born James Patrick Yorkston Wright, 21 December 1971, Stratford-upon-Avon, England; raised in Kingsbarns/Cellardyke, Fife, Scotland · active
  • Members: Solo artist; core member of the Fence Collective (with King Creosote, KT Tunstall, The Beta Band, The Pictish Trail); frequent collaborator with Second Hand Orchestra, Nina Persson, and the Yorkston/Thorne/Khan trio

James Yorkston — A Moment Longer

Played: 2026-07-30

"A Moment Longer" features Swedish singer Nina Persson (of The Cardigans) and appears on James Yorkston's 2025 album Songs for Nina and Johanna, released 22 August 2025 via Domino Recording Company. The album pairs the Scottish folk singer-songwriter with two Swedish vocal icons — Persson and Johanna Söderberg (of First Aid Kit). Yorkston has attributed the album to "heightened emotions" during the recording sessions, and press notes mention that while recording "A Moment Longer," the chemistry between Yorkston and Persson was particularly palpable. The track was one of two advance singles (alongside "Love / Luck" with Söderberg).
