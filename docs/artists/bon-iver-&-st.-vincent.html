Bon Iver

  • Origin: Eau Claire, Wisconsin, USA · formed 2006 · active
  • Members: Justin Vernon (vocals, guitar, production — primary/founding member); the live/recording lineup has also included Sean Carey, Michael Lewis, Matthew McCaughan, and others
  • Genre: Indie folk, experimental folk, art pop
  • Discography:
  • For Emma, Forever Ago (2007, self-released; wide release 2008 on Jagjaguwar) — the cabin-recorded debut that built the band's founding mythology
  • Bon Iver, Bon Iver (2011, Jagjaguwar) — US #2, won the Grammy for Best Alternative Music Album
  • 22, A Million (2016, Jagjaguwar) — US #2, a sharp turn toward glitchy, processed textures
  • i,i (2019, Jagjaguwar)
  • Facts:
  • Justin Vernon was born 30 April 1981 in Eau Claire, Wisconsin, and still lives there; the "For Emma" origin story (a winter alone in his father's hunting cabin) became the project's defining myth.
  • "Rosyln," the featured track here, is a collaboration with Annie Clark (St. Vincent) of Dallas, Texas, recorded for the Twilight: New Moon soundtrack (2009) — one of several high-profile outside collaborations for Vernon, alongside Big Red Machine (with Aaron Dessner) and production/writing work for Kanye West.
