Bruce Springsteen — The Ghost of Tom Joad

Played: 2026-07-09

Title track of Springsteen's stark, largely acoustic 1995 album. Directly invokes Tom Joad from Steinbeck's The Grapes of Wrath (and Woody Guthrie's own "Tom Joad" ballad), recasting Depression-era displacement into a portrait of 1990s American poverty. Later became a signature Rage Against the Machine cover, giving it a second life for a different generation.

Bruce Springsteen

  • Origin: Freehold/Long Branch, New Jersey, US · born September 23, 1949 · active since the mid-1960s
  • Members: Solo artist, most often recording and touring with the E Street Band (past and present members include Clarence Clemons, Max Weinberg, Roy Bittan, Steven Van Zandt, Nils Lofgren, Patti Scialfa, Garry Tallent, and Danny Federici)
  • Genre: Rock, heartland rock, folk rock
  • Discography:
  • Born to Run (1975) — Columbia
  • Darkness on the Edge of Town (1978) — Columbia
  • The River (1980) — Columbia
  • Nebraska (1982) — Columbia — stark solo acoustic record
  • Born in the U.S.A. (1984) — Columbia — one of the best-selling albums of all time
  • Tunnel of Love (1987) — Columbia
  • The Ghost of Tom Joad (1995) — Columbia
  • Facts:
  • The E Street Band takes its name from a street in Belmar, NJ, where keyboardist David Sancious's mother lived and where the band held some of its earliest rehearsals.
