AI Policy

Less Than Zero uses AI for research and logistics — background on artists and records, tracklist formatting, and production admin. This speeds up the work that would otherwise take hours of manual searching, and frees time for the creative decisions that make the show.

The following are always done by Chris:

  • Tracklist ordering and sequencing
  • Writing scripts and social copy
  • Tile art and visual identity
  • A Moment in History research

AI never receives creative credit. It is a tool for the backend work of a one-person show.

Less Than Zero broadcasts to a UK audience.
